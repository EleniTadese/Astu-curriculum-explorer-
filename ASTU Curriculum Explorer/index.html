About This Website

The ASTU Curriculum Explorer was developed to simplify the process of exploring academic programs at Adama Science and Technology University.

We understand that navigating university curriculum can be challenging, so we created this website to provide a user-friendly and centralized resource for students to find the information they need.

This website allows you to:

Easily browse through different academic streams (Engineering and Applied Science)

Quickly find the departments and sub-departments (for Engineering) that interest you.

Access detailed curriculum information (coming soon!).

Plan your academic journey more effectively.
